These MIDI files are the notes the game plays. Each track is assigned to one of the five sampled sections.

圣者进行曲（旧版自由模式）
  File: saints.mid
  Source: Wikimedia Commons, File:When_The_Saints.mid. Traditional melody.
  Setting by Rabanus Flavus / Peter Gerloff. CC0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:When_The_Saints.mid
  The game loops the first 16 bars of 4/4, starting where the melody enters.
  Sax, guitar or piano play that melody when they solo. Bass uses this file's bass.
  Drums are a march pulse, not a new tune.
